Five worlds to learn, and a home for your creations.

- Thirty offline lessons in five worlds, with challenge checks, three hints and stars.
- Daily challenges, bug hunts, prediction quizzes, badges and world certificates.
- Searchable reference, glossary, line explanations, progress reports and a Python track.
- Fifty categorized examples, six project templates and replay for individual tour parts.
- Projects on Home with stage thumbnails, rename, duplicate and move to Trash.
- Autosave and crash recovery protect drafts; saved versions keep your newer work.
- Portable project bundles and an Assets panel for images and sounds.
- Share cards and Make an App with your own name, icon and fullscreen player.

This beta is a small code pack for the existing Sparky 3 runtime.
Stable website downloads remain at 2.0 until Sparky 3.0 is finished.
Physical Arduino uploads and the Windows installer on a real PC remain untested.
